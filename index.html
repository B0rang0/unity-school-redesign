The Unity Difference

Mind. Body. Spirit.

Unity School's mission is to empower children and inspire learning by educating the entire child. Students are challenged in a supportive environment while families, faculty and staff work together as one commUNITY.

Mind

Strong academics, curiosity, creativity and critical thinking.

Body

Movement, athletics, wellness and healthy lifelong habits.

Spirit

Compassion, peace, character, service and respect for others.
