The Montessori Experience

Learning Through Discovery.

Unity's Montessori classrooms promote self-discipline, responsibility and independence. Child-sized furnishings and intentionally organized materials allow children to select work, practice concentration and build confidence under thoughtful teacher guidance.

The Montessori Method

Designed for Young Learners.

Materials are organized into purposeful work areas and designed to be self-correcting, encouraging children to learn through practice and discovery.

As students develop order, coordination, concentration and independence, they become increasingly confident and self-reliant.
