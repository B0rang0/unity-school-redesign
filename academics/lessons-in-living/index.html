Beyond Academics

Character Is Part of the Curriculum.

Unity devotes an entire department to nurturing students, staff and families through values centered on peace, acceptance, tolerance, compassion, non-violence and reverence for life.

As a non-denominational school, Unity also encourages appreciation for religious and cultural diversity.
